Road signs · TPDM Vol 3

Placed signs

Click a sign in the view or in Placed signs to select it; × in the list or Delete / Backspace removes it, Esc deselects. R / Shift+R turns it 15°. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Plates (714) hang under the lowest sign; other signs stack above.

Street furniture · HyD Section 2

Placed

Click the ground to place an item anywhere; railings, barriers and rope bollards take two clicks (start, end). Click a placed item to select it, then drag it to move. Arrow keys nudge 0.1 m (Shift: 1 m), R / Shift+R turns 15°, Delete removes, Esc deselects, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes. Saved per layout in this browser.
